What makes a suitable vending location?

To ensure optimal service and viability, we assess locations based on specific criteria. Meeting these baselines ensures a successful, zero-hassle partnership.

group

Footfall

Consistent daily traffic of 100+ individuals (staff, residents, or visitors).

power

Power Supply

Access to a standard 13-amp wall socket within 2 metres of the machine.

signal_cellular_alt

Connectivity

Adequate cellular signal or available Wi-Fi for telemetry and smart payments.

door_front

Accessibility

Clear, step-free access for installation and regular restocking teams.
