General

What is VendiSmart?

expand_more
VendiSmart provides modern, tech-forward vending solutions for workplaces. We replace outdated machines with smart, connected kiosks that offer fresh, healthy, and traditional refreshment options with seamless cashless payments.

Who is VendiSmart for?

expand_more
Our solutions are ideal for offices, manufacturing facilities, hospitals, universities, and any location looking to provide hassle-free, modern refreshment options for their staff or visitors.

Installation

How much space is required?

expand_more
A standard VendiSmart machine requires approximately 1 square meter of floor space. We offer various machine sizes to fit different environments. Our team will conduct a site survey to recommend the best fit.

What are the electrical requirements?

expand_more
Our machines require a standard 13-amp power socket nearby. They are highly energy-efficient, utilizing smart cooling and LED lighting to minimize power consumption.
